# 09 – Legal Page Templates

> Templates only. Have a lawyer or the shop's advisor review before publishing. Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]`.
> Host the pages on a public HTTPS site (e.g. `https://<domain>/privacy`). Provide Tamil versions too.

## A. Privacy Policy (template)
**Effective date:** [DATE] · **Operator:** Sun Flowers and Decoration, [FULL ADDRESS], Tamil Nadu, India · **Contact:** [EMAIL], [PHONE]

1. **What we collect**
   - Account: name, mobile number, email (if Google sign-in).
   - Delivery: addresses, location coordinates you select on the map, landmark, recipient name and phone.
   - Orders: items, amounts, payment method type, order status, delivery notes.
   - Device: push notification token, app version, device model/OS, crash and diagnostic logs.
   - Payments: handled by Cashfree. We do not receive or store your card/UPI credentials.
2. **Why we collect it**: to create your account, check delivery availability, process and deliver orders, send order updates, provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, improve app stability.
3. **Location**: we use your location only while the app is open, only when you choose "Use current location" or pick a point on the map. We do not track your location in the background.
4. **Sharing**: Firebase/Google (authentication, notifications, storage, crash reporting), Cashfree (payments), Google Maps (maps/geocoding), our hosting/database providers (Neon, [HOST]) as processors; our delivery staff receive name, phone, address of your order. We do not sell personal data.
5. **Retention**: account data while your account is active; order records are retained as required by law for accounting/tax in minimal form after account deletion; logs up to 30 days.
6. **Security**: encryption in transit, access controls, regular reviews. No system is 100% secure.
7. **Your rights**: access, correction, deletion, withdraw consent, grievance redressal. Delete your account in the app (Account > Delete Account) or at [DELETION URL]. We act on requests within [30] days.
8. **Children**: the app is not directed to children under 18.
9. **Changes**: we will notify you in the app of material changes.
10. **Grievance Officer**: [NAME], [EMAIL], [PHONE], response within [15] days.
